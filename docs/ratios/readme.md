# Ratio Catalog

## Facility Maintenance Industry Key Financial Ratios

| Category | Ratio | Description | Formula |
|----------|-------|-------------|---------|
| Profitability | Gross Profit Margin | Measures profit after covering direct costs | (Revenue - Cost of Services Provided) / Revenue * 100 |
| | Net Profit Margin | Shows percentage of revenue remaining as profit after all expenses | Net Income / Revenue * 100 |
| | Operating Profit Margin (EBIT Margin) | Reflects percentage of revenue remaining after covering operating expenses | Operating Income / Revenue * 100 |
| Efficiency | Labor Utilization Rate | Measures efficiency of labor usage | Billable Hours / Total Available Hours * 100 |
| | Revenue per Technician | Assesses productivity of maintenance staff | Revenue / Number of Technicians |
| | Work Order Completion Rate | Measures efficiency of completing maintenance tasks | Number of Completed Work Orders / Total Work Orders * 100 |
| Liquidity | Current Ratio | Evaluates ability to pay short-term obligations | Current Assets / Current Liabilities |
| | Quick Ratio (Acid-Test Ratio) | More stringent measure of liquidity | (Current Assets - Inventory) / Current Liabilities |
| Solvency | Debt-to-Equity Ratio | Indicates proportion of financing from creditors and investors | Total Liabilities / Total Equity |
| | Interest Coverage Ratio | Measures ability to pay interest on debt | Operating Income / Interest Expense |
| Customer & Service | Customer Retention Rate | Indicates effectiveness in maintaining client base | (Number of Customers at End of Period - New Customers Acquired) / Number of Customers at Start of Period * 100 |
| | Average Contract Value (ACV) | Reflects average value of maintenance contracts | Total Revenue from Contracts / Number of Contracts |
| Cost Management | Materials Cost Percentage | Measures percentage of revenue spent on materials and supplies | Cost of Materials / Revenue * 100 |
| | Overhead Cost Percentage | Indicates proportion of total expenses from overhead costs | Overhead Costs / Total Expenses * 100 |
| Growth | Revenue Growth Rate | Tracks increase in revenue over a period | (Revenue in Current Period - Revenue in Previous Period) / Revenue in Previous Period * 100 |
| | Net Income Growth Rate | Shows growth in net income over a period | (Net Income in Current Period - Net Income in Previous Period) / Net Income in Previous Period * 100 |

## Financial Ratio Interpretations

| Ratio | Computation | Example | Meaning |
|-------|-------------|---------|---------|
| Current Ratio | Current Assets / Current Liabilities | 1.50 | I have $1.50 in current assets to pay every dollar in current liabilities. |
| Quick Ratio | (Cash + Accounts Receivable) / Current Liabilities | .85 | I have $.85 in quick assets (liquid) to pay every dollar in current liabilities. |
| Debt to Equity | Total Liabilities / Equity | 2.75 | For every dollar I (the owner) put into the business, the creditors have put in $2.75. |
| Gross Profit Margin | Gross Profit / Sales | 35.0% | For every dollar of sales I generate, I make 35¢ at the gross profit level. |
| Pretax Margin | Pretax Profit / Sales | 3.0% | For every dollar of sales I generate, I make 3¢ at the pre-tax profit level. |
| Sales to Assets | Sales / Total Assets | 2.60 | For every $1.00 in assets in my business, I generate $2.60 in sales. |
| Return on Assets (ROA) | Pretax Profit / Total Assets | 10.0% | For every dollar in assets in my business, I generate 10¢ in profits. |
| Return on Equity (ROI) | Pretax Profit / Equity | 27.0% | For every dollar I've invested in the business, I generate a return of 27¢. |
| Inventory Turnover | Cost of Goods Sold / Inventory | 6 X | On average, I turn my inventory 6 times a year. |
| Days in Inventory | 365 days / Inventory Turnover | 61 days | On average I turn my inventory every 61 days. |
| Accounts Receivable Turnover | Sales / Accounts Receivable | 9 X | On average I collect my accounts receivable 9 times a year. |
| Collection Period | 365 days / Accounts Receivable Turnover | 41 days | On average I collect my accounts receivable every 41 days. |
| Accounts Payable Turnover | Cost of Goods Sold / Accounts Payable | 7 X | On average I pay my accounts payable 7 times a year. |
| Payable Period | 365 / Accounts Payable Turnover | 52 days | On average I pay my accounts payable every 52 days. |
| Cash Flow to Profit | Operating Cash Flow / Pretax Profit | 34.0% | For every dollar of profit before tax, I generate 34¢ in cash flow. |
| Cash Flow Debt Coverage | Interest Bearing Liabilities / Cash Flow Before Financing | 4.6X | It will take 4.6 years to retire existing interest bearing debt. |
| Cash Flow Return on Assets | Operating Cash Flow / Total Assets | 8.7% | For every dollar of assets, I generate 8.7¢ in operating cash flow. |
| Cash Return on Equity | Operating Cash Flow / Equity | 29.4% | For every dollar of equity, I generate 29.4¢ in operating cash flow. |

    
## Bookkeeping KPIs 

| KPI | Description | Formula | Target |
|-----|-------------|---------|--------|
| 1. Accuracy of Financial Records | Measures the precision of the financial data being recorded. | Number of errors found / Total number of entries | Typically, a lower percentage is better, aiming for near-zero errors |
| 2. Timeliness of Data Entry | Assesses how quickly financial transactions are recorded. | Average time taken to record a transaction after it occurs | Data should be entered within 24-48 hours of the transaction |
| 3. Accounts Receivable Turnover | Evaluates how efficiently receivables are collected. | Net Credit Sales / Average Accounts Receivable | A higher ratio indicates faster collection of receivables |
| 4. Accounts Payable Turnover | Measures the rate at which the company pays its suppliers. | Total Supplier Purchases / Average Accounts Payable | A lower ratio might indicate better cash flow management, but it should be balanced against the need to maintain good supplier relationships |
| 5. Bank Reconciliation Completion Time | Tracks how quickly bank reconciliations are completed after the month-end. | Days taken to complete bank reconciliation after month-end | Should be completed within 5-7 days after the month-end |
| 6. Expense Variance | Compares actual expenses to budgeted expenses. | (Actual Expenses - Budgeted Expenses) / Budgeted Expenses | The variance should be minimal, indicating accurate budgeting and control |
| 7. Compliance with Tax Deadlines | Measures the timeliness of tax filings and payments. | Number of tax deadlines met / Total number of tax deadlines | 100% compliance |
| 8. Cash Flow Forecast Accuracy | Assesses how close cash flow forecasts are to actual results. | (Forecasted Cash Flow - Actual Cash Flow) / Forecasted Cash Flow | A lower percentage indicates more accurate forecasting |
| 9. Cost per Invoice Processed | Evaluates the cost-effectiveness of the invoicing process. | Total Costs of Processing Invoices / Number of Invoices Processed | Lower costs per invoice indicate more efficient processing |
| 10. Customer Invoice Error Rate | Measures the number of errors in customer invoicing. | Number of Invoice Errors / Total Number of Invoices Sent | A lower percentage is better, aiming for minimal errors |

    
## Industry-Specific Ratios

| Industry | Ratio | Measures |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Retail | Sales / Retail Selling Space Square Footage | Volume indicator that measures the sales dollar generated from the square footage of retail selling space |
| Retail | Sales by Individual / Individual's Direct Cost | Measures individual's productivity; indicates whether a sales person is paying for him/herself |
| Retail | Rent Expense / Sales | Measures rent expense as a percent of sales; indicates whether expense is staying in line with sales production |
| Wholesale/Distributors | Wholesale/Delivery Expense / Sales | Measures the wholesale/delivery expense as a percent of sales; monitors expense over time |
| Wholesale/Distributors | Product Line Gross Profit / Product Sales | Measures individual product line's Gross Profit Margin; helps measure product line's contribution to operating expense and profit |
| Wholesale/Distributors | Product Inventory Turn / Product's Gross Profit % | Measures gross return on inventory dollars by product line; enables ranking of lines by gross return |
| Service | Billable Hours / Available Billable Hours | Measures individual's productivity; compares time actually billing customers to non-productive time |
| Service | Billable Revenue / Salary Expense | Measures whether revenue producers are paying for themselves |
| Service | Total Billable Fees / Billable Hours | Measures average effective billing rate; can measure against actual billable rate for efficiency |
| Service | Operating Expense / Sales | Measures overhead expense as a percent of sales; indicates if operating expenses are staying in line with sales |
| Manufacturers | Direct Labor / Sales | Measures productivity of labor; reveals how much direct labor expense is incurred for a dollar of sales |
| Manufacturers | Direct Materials / Sales | Measures materials' cost of sales; can measure cost of materials in relationship to sales over time |
| Manufacturers | Shop Overhead / Sales | Measures overhead expense in relation to sales; tracks expense and determines proper management |
| Contractors | Cost of Goods Sold / Work in Process | Measures turnover of work in process; shows how efficiently work in process is managed when compared year to year |
| Contractors | Individual Job Gross Profit / Job Revenue | Measures individual job's Gross Profit Margin; provides measurement against company goals or standards |
| Contractors | Actual Job Cost / Bid Job Cost | Measures ability to effectively bid a job and manage its costs; calculation less than one indicates job came in under bid |
